The Reward System

HAY, Cheese & the Ecosystem Bridge

GOATflow turns completed work into a persistent, portable record of output. Every Track completed earns HAY. HAY converts to Fresh Cheese. Fresh Cheese ports to WorkGOAT — the companion credentialing platform — at 1:1 on launch. Users arrive with proof of work already banked.

Action HAY Earned Notes
Standard Track Completed +10 HAY Base reward for any completed Track
Summit Call Completed +50 HAY High-priority flagged items earn 5× base
Speed Bonus +25 HAY Completed within time estimate
Difficulty Multiplier Variable Based on personal completion history — the system learns what is hard for you
500 HAY → 1 Fresh Cheese Persistent Asset Ports to WorkGOAT at 1:1 on launch
