About

An Operations Leader Who Builds His Own Tools

For over 12 years, I have led complex, multi-site logistics operations across both coasts — stabilizing distressed markets, managing compliance across 10,000+ service addresses, and directing enterprise-scale route auditing programs. I was promoted to Regional Operations Director and relocated cross-country to Washington State, where I earned recognition as a FY2025 Top Performer for enterprise route auditing and remote location support.

What sets me apart is not just operational depth — it is what I do with it. I independently build AI-powered tools to solve the operational problems I encounter firsthand. FlashRT, GOATflow, NayAye, and The DEB are not side projects. They are applied solutions to real inefficiencies, built by someone who understands the problem from the ground up.

I am now targeting remote operations, program management, and AI enablement roles in the private sector — bringing a rare combination of field-tested leadership and hands-on technical capability.
