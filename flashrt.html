FlashRT
AI-Guided Carrier Onboarding Technology
FlashRT eliminates the single biggest operational bottleneck in field logistics: the time it takes a new carrier to learn a route. By combining intelligent route sequencing, real-time field coaching, and adaptive performance benchmarking, FlashRT compresses the onboarding cycle from weeks to days — and turns a recurring cost center into a measurable competitive advantage.

The Problem This Solves

Built From 12 Years of Watching Onboarding Fail

FlashRT was not conceived in a boardroom. It was conceived after years of watching the same problem repeat itself across multiple facilities: a new carrier arrives, is handed a case and a map, and spends the next three to six weeks learning by trial and error — generating complaints, missing delivery windows, and burning supervisor time that could be deployed elsewhere.

The problem is not the carrier. The problem is the absence of a structured, intelligent guidance system that meets them where they are — in the field, in real time, with context-specific coaching. FlashRT is that system.

The concept was formally documented as a Concept Overview & Commercial Value Assessment in 2025, including a full analysis of addressable market, implementation pathway, and projected ROI at scale.

Core System Architecture

How FlashRT Works

Intelligent Route Sequencing
FlashRT analyzes route structure, delivery density, and case layout to generate an optimized learning sequence — prioritizing the highest-complexity segments early and building carrier confidence systematically.
Real-Time Field Coaching
Context-sensitive guidance delivered at the point of need — not in a classroom the week before. Carriers receive prompts, reminders, and corrections as they work the route, not after the fact.
Adaptive Performance Benchmarking
FlashRT tracks individual carrier progress against route-specific benchmarks — not generic standards — and adjusts coaching intensity based on where each carrier is in their learning curve.
Supervisor Intelligence Layer
Supervisors receive a real-time view of new carrier progress, flagged risk areas, and projected time-to-proficiency — replacing gut-feel check-ins with data-driven intervention triggers.
Why This Matters Now

The Operational Context

The Problem at Scale
  • Carrier turnover in logistics operations runs 30–50% annually at many facilities
  • Each new hire represents 3–6 weeks of below-standard performance and supervisor overhead
  • Route changes — reassignments, coverage, new routes — trigger the same learning curve repeatedly
  • Current onboarding is largely informal, undocumented, and supervisor-dependent
  • No existing system captures route-specific institutional knowledge in a transferable format
What FlashRT Changes
  • Converts tacit route knowledge into structured, transferable AI-guided content
  • Reduces supervisor time-on-task during onboarding by 40–60% (estimated)
  • Creates a documented performance record from day one of field deployment
  • Scales across any route network without proportional cost increase
  • Generates compounding value: every hire, every route change, every facility

The concept is grounded in operational reality — not theoretical efficiency modeling.
