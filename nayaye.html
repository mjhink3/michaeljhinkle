The Problem It Solves
The AI Tool Landscape Is a Maze

There are hundreds of AI tools available today — and the number grows every week. The question is never "does an AI exist that can do this?" The question is: which one should I use, right now, for this specific task, given my constraints?

NayAye answers that question. It takes your task, your skill level, your budget, your privacy requirements, and your desired output format — and routes you to the optimal tool with a clear explanation of why. No jargon. No vendor bias. No wasted time.

Built and deployed independently on Vercel. The old-timey surveyor theme is intentional — navigating the AI landscape requires the same instincts as navigating unfamiliar territory: know your tools, trust your read of the situation, and ride toward the right answer.

How It Works
Three Questions. One Routing Decision.
What's the Task?
Describe what you need to accomplish — writing, analysis, image generation, code, research, automation, or anything else. Plain language. No technical jargon required.
What Are Your Constraints?
Skill level, budget, privacy sensitivity, and output format all factor into the routing decision. NayAye weighs them all simultaneously — not sequentially.
Receive Your Route
A clear recommendation: which tool to use, why it fits your situation, and how to get started immediately. No hedging. No "it depends." A route.
