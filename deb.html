The Problem It Solves

Information Overload Is a Leadership Problem

Leaders, operators, and founders spend hours every morning scanning feeds, newsletters, and dashboards — trying to extract the three things that actually matter. Most of that time is wasted. The signal is buried in noise, and the noise keeps growing.

The DEB solves this with a fully automated AI pipeline built on Gumloop. You configure it once — your email, your preferred delivery time, your topics of interest, your source feeds — and every morning you receive a structured brief that surfaces the top signals, adds the "why it matters" context, and flags forward-looking indicators.

No coding required. No ongoing maintenance. The system runs itself. And it was designed to be built by anyone — in 15 minutes — using the guide and prompt template included in the published framework.

How It Works

Four Steps. Fifteen Minutes. Fully Automated.

01
Start Building
Create a free Gumloop account. Click "Build Your First Agent" to launch the workflow builder interface.
02
Paste the Prompt
Paste the provided master build prompt into the AI assistant. The system interprets your instructions and generates the complete workflow structure automatically.
03
Answer 4 Questions
Your email. Your preferred delivery time. Three topics to monitor. Five source feeds to scan. The AI can suggest sources if you're unsure.
04
Run & Automate
Test the flow manually. If it fails, click "Fix with AI." Once verified, the brief generates automatically every morning — no further action required.

"The finished product is not a newsletter. It is an intelligence brief — structured, contextual, and forward-looking. Built for operators who need to know what matters before the day starts."
