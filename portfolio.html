Introduction

Three Chapters. One Operating Philosophy.

This portfolio documents twelve years of operational leadership across two coasts — from frontline field specialist to Regional Operations Director managing multi-site logistics networks in some of the most complex delivery environments in the Pacific Northwest.

Each chapter represents a distinct phase of professional growth: Chapter One covers the Washington State years — Vashon Island, Preston, and the enterprise audit program. Chapter Two covers the Maple Falls stabilization — a compound adversity environment requiring operational endurance and workforce rebuilding. Chapter Three covers the Pennsylvania foundation — where the operational philosophy was built.

Running in parallel with all three chapters is a growing portfolio of independently developed AI tools and automation systems — built from the same operational experience documented here.

Vashon Island — Market Stabilization
Inherited a chronically distressed remote island market facing a pending route pay reduction that threatened to destabilize the entire carrier workforce. Developed and deployed two custom workforce education documents — an Algorithmic Compensation Guide and a KPI Quick Guide — that educated carriers on the performance-based compensation system, defused the crisis, and converted a workforce on the verge of attrition into a stable, high-performing team. Maintained 99.8% compliance across 10,000+ addresses throughout the stabilization period.

Chapter Three — Foundation June 2013 – September 2022
Pennsylvania
Systems Optimization, Process Innovation & Early Leadership Recognition
Executive Summary
Advanced from Field Operations Specialist supporting 20+ routes across 8 offices to Regular status managing three full-time routes — consistently delivering high-efficiency, data-optimized performance, mentoring incoming team members, and earning repeated selection for executive interim director assignments prior to promotion to Regional Operations Director. Cross-country relocation to Washington State executed upon promotion.
Operational Scale & Efficiency
As a Field Operations Specialist, provided coverage across 20+ routes spanning 8 offices — frequently carrying multiple routes in a single day and serving as one of the most deployable relief specialists in the district. On a 6.5-hour evaluated route, averaged approximately 3 hours actual delivery time, generating an estimated 750–800 annualized operational capacity hours that increased vehicle availability and scheduling flexibility across the office complement.
This efficiency was not incidental — it was the product of deliberate route study, scanning discipline, and customer familiarity built across years of consistent coverage. That same operational philosophy carried forward into every subsequent chapter.

Data Optimization & Algorithmic Compensation
As a performance-based compensation system was implemented across the district, studied and applied precise scanner documentation practices — trip-to-door scanning, authorized dismount documentation, multi-trip parcel logging, and hold mail delivery documentation — that increased evaluations across all three full-time routes, elevated route classifications, and improved compensation accuracy. Coached peers on compliance-based optimization as the system became operational.
This early data optimization expertise later became the foundation for the workforce education documents deployed at Vashon Island — the Algorithmic Compensation Guide and KPI Quick Guide that defused a route pay reduction crisis in Chapter One.
Process Innovation
Redesigned the parcel locker key system for a 50% CBU route section — improving retrieval transparency, reducing customer confusion, and decreasing potential complaint volume. Identified and resolved route technology and scanning issues that were creating operational continuity gaps and customer claim exposure, improving both data integrity and service reliability.
Interim Director — Four Executive Detail Assignments
Selected by senior leadership for four separate one-week Interim Director assignments between 2021 and 2022 — assuming full-site operational authority for a 2,000+ address distributed logistics hub during leadership leave periods. Each assignment included staffing oversight, scheduling, retail operations, financial controls, and compliance management for an 8-person facility. Maintained 100% service continuity and zero safety incidents across all four details.
Mentored and trained 5+ new field specialists during the carrier tenure, accelerating onboarding and reinforcing compliance and safety standards. The repeated selection for executive details and peer mentorship role directly supported the case for promotion — and led to the cross-country relocation to Washington State as Regional Operations Director.
